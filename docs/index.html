Abstract

In this study we present, to the best of our knowledge, the first real-sensor RGB-X multi-view, multi-modality image matching dataset for driving scenes. Matching images across sensor modalities is a prerequisite for perception systems that pair an RGB camera with a thermal or infrared one, yet it is evaluated almost entirely on wide-baseline, static, clear-weather benchmarks. Driving violates all three assumptions, and method rankings obtained under clear conditions reverse under rain and snow. Existing resources do not close this gap: pre-aligned collections offer no viewpoint change, synthetic cross-modality data inherits landmark viewpoints, and driving multi-sensor datasets release inertial poses rather than matching supervision. Nor can such supervision simply be reconstructed: Structure-from-Motion followed by multi-view stereo becomes ill-conditioned under near-parallel forward motion, dynamic objects and low-texture weather. We therefore build a reconstruction pipeline for this setting. Intrinsics are fixed from the sensor specification and never bundle-adjusted, dynamic content is removed by geometric consistency, and reconstruction escalates adaptively on low-texture scenes under explicit quality gating, so that unreliable scenes are excluded rather than released as noisy ground truth. Released poses are verified against two independent matchers (median rotation error 0.094°). Applied to RGB-SWIR driving imagery, the pipeline yields SPECTRA: 384,550 verified cross-modality pairs with poses and depth. Fine-tuning on SPECTRA improves every architecture tested on unseen RGB-thermal data, across two platforms and two metrics, with margins that grow as conditions degrade.

Reconstruction pipeline

Structure-from-Motion

COLMAP on the RGB stream with intrinsics fixed from the sensor specification and never bundle-adjusted. Low-texture scenes escalate to stronger features, and every scene passes an explicit quality gate.

Pose densification

Frames left out of the initial reconstruction are registered against the accepted model, with existing poses and intrinsics held fixed.

Multi-view stereo

Patch-match stereo followed by MegaDepth-style depth refinement. Dynamic objects are kept only where they are geometrically consistent, and sky is removed.

Verified pairs

Pair overlap is recomputed from the depth maps with a mutual depth-consistency check, and only well-overlapping pairs are released. SWIR frames reuse the RGB pose and depth.

The full pipeline runs with one command and works for any pre-aligned RGB-X sequence. See the code.

Unseen modality: RGB → thermal

SPECTRA is RGB-SWIR, but we evaluate on RGB-thermal: the fine-tuned models never see a thermal image during training. On DSERT, fine-tuning on SPECTRA improves every model on its own baseline in every weather condition, and the margin widens as conditions get worse.
